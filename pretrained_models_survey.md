## Top 5 to try next on the UR5 cell

1. **Cosmos-Policy (ALOHA/LIBERO ckpts — already cached + env ready).** Only downloaded policy with a genuine model-based *planning* mode: it predicts actions AND future proprio+images AND a value, then does best-of-N search. That is the blog's Loop-2 story in a single model — "imagine futures, pick the best" vs GR00T-H's blind reactive chunks.
2. **jepa-wms pretrained JEPA-WMs (+ V-JEPA2.1 encoder upgrade on our V-JEPA2-AC probe).** We already have V-JEPA2-AC running zero-shot on UR5 traces; jepa-wms gives DROID-trained latent planners with CEM code and reports beating V-JEPA2-AC — a direct, cheap upgrade path to real closed-loop latent MPC on the cell (the probe's stated next step).
3. **MolmoAct2-DROID** (`allenai/MolmoAct2-DROID`, 5B, Apache-2.0). Newest fully-open VLA with a DROID EE-delta checkpoint, and its ARM design emits an interpretable spatial plan (depth + trajectory trace) before acting — perfect for the "reasoned plan vs reactive chunk" figure.
4. **Cosmos3-Edge-Policy-DROID** (4B, cached, ungated, measured ~8.7 GB). One download-free server launch gives a DROID-tuned policy; the same Cosmos3-Edge backbone then doubles as an action-conditioned forward-dynamics WAM — policy and world model in one checkpoint family.
5. **DreamZero-DROID** (14B, CC-BY-NC). It *is* the GR00T-N2 architecture (world action model predicting video+actions jointly, 7 Hz closed-loop on GB200). On GB10 expect seconds-per-chunk — not real-time, but a headline demo: "the thing NVIDIA says beats VLAs 2×, running on a desk." Non-commercial license is fine for a research blog.
   *Honorable mention:* **LeWAM** — 0.4 B, has BOTH a policy and a diffusion-steering MPC mode; cheapest possible MPC-vs-policy A/B.

## Planning vs reactive — one-line summary
Real MPC/planning modes ship with: V-JEPA2-AC (CEM), jepa-wms JEPA-WMs + DINO-WM (CEM), PLDM/HWM (MPPI), LeWAM (diffusion-steering MPC), RoboJEPA (goal-latent planning), Cosmos-Policy (best-of-N value search), DreamZero + Cosmos3 WAM modes (video rollout scoring), NanoWM (MPC), our own dynamics-MLP MPC. Everything else on the list (GR00T family, π*, OpenVLA/OFT, Octo, RDT, SmolVLA{,2}, CogACT, DexVLA, TinyVLA, X-VLA, LingBot, MolmoAct1/2, GR00T-DROID variants) is a reactive action-chunk policy — MolmoAct's spatial trace is the only thing close to "deliberation" among them.
